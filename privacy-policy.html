Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Summary: FreeMusicDownload.site does not require user registration, does not collect personal identifying information (PII), and operates on a strict zero-logging policy. All video and audio processing is transient and temporarily streamed without permanent storage.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to FreeMusicDownload.site ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent online experience. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you access our website, use our Free Music Download tool, or utilize our social media video downloaders for TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, and Snapchat.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike many online converters and web services, we respect your personal autonomy:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, provide your name, enter your email address, or submit phone numbers to use our service.
  • No Payment Details: Our tool is 100% free; we never request credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • No Download History: We do not associate URLs you submit with your identity, nor do we maintain persistent profiles of what videos or songs you download.

3. Transient URL Processing & File Retention

When you paste a media link into our downloader:

  1. Our server connects directly to the source media URL to inspect available streams (resolving watermark-free MP4 or high-bitrate MP3 audio).
  2. The media is delivered to your browser via real-time streaming buffers.
  3. Any transient temporary cache created on our backend server is governed by an automated cleanup script that purges files older than 60 minutes. We do not maintain a permanent archive of downloaded content.

We may use basic functional cookies to remember user preferences (such as your chosen platform filter). In addition, third-party partners like Google AdSense or analytics providers may use cookies, web beacons, or unique device identifiers to serve relevant advertisements and analyze site traffic.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on FreeMusicDownload.site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Log Files & Server Telemetry

Like virtually all web applications, our web hosting servers automatically record standard non-identifying telemetry in log files. This data includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamps, and basic performance metrics. We use this aggregated information solely for server diagnostic analysis, DDoS mitigation, and capacity planning.

6. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website provides convenience links that allow you to open third-party platforms directly (such as TikTok.com, YouTube.com, and Instagram.com). Please note that once you navigate away from FreeMusicDownload.site, you are subject to the privacy practices and terms of that external website.

7. GDPR Compliance (European Union Users)

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), we adhere to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect personal data, you can freely browse and download media without risking your privacy rights. To the extent any processing of network IP addresses occurs, our lawful basis is legitimate interest in securing our web infrastructure.

8. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure of data collection categories and the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal data. FreeMusicDownload.site does not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to third parties.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

FreeMusicDownload.site does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal information on our site, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy & Contact
